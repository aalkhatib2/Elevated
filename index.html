The Ladder

Four rungs. No mystery.

Most comp plans are explained once, in a room, by someone who benefits from you misremembering it. This one is written down and it does not move.

EL
Entry Level

You sell, you get paid on what you sell. Everyone starts here and nobody skips it, including the people running divisions today.

STL
Sales Team Leader

Your own production plus the first reps you bring in. This is the first rung where your team pays you, not just your own knocks.

STC
Sales Team Captain

A team deep enough to run without you in every conversation. You are coaching leaders now, not just closing your own doors.

JP
Junior Partner

You are building the organization instead of working inside it. The top of the published ladder, and the start of a different conversation.

Advancement is your production plus the team you have built. Exact thresholds are published per division and given to you in writing on day one.

This is not for everyone

Plenty of people do well in this work. Not all of them do well here. The reps who last tend to look like this.

You want the numbers in writing

If a comp plan has to be explained in a hype video, you are already suspicious. Reasonable instinct.

You would rather be measured than managed

Nobody is going to stand behind you. Your production is visible, and that cuts both ways.

You are building a team, not just a check

The ladder pays on depth. If you never want anyone under you, EL is a fine place to stay, and it should be your plan.

You knock when the weather is bad

The job is mostly this. Everything else on this page is downstream of whether you go out on the ugly days.
